# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sales team members use LeadFlow to manage prospects and keep lead follow-ups moving. The user confirmed that daily lead follow-up and the workbench are the mobile priorities.

## Product Purpose

LeadFlow supports the sales workflow from lead intake and tracking through follow-up, scheduled activities, conversion reporting, and team oversight.

## Positioning

The product combines lead tracking with a daily workbench and follow-up queue. A distinct market-positioning claim is undecided.

## Operating Context

The app serves role-scoped sales and management workflows. Lead follow-up, calls, meetings, scheduled tasks, and team activity are part of day-to-day use. Business dates and activity times use Asia/Dhaka semantics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include dashboard metrics, daily workbench, lead search and updates, lead intake, follow-up queue, activity scheduling, lead details, team and user management, reports, settings, and notifications. Route access, data visibility, and actions are permission-controlled. The user requested a responsive web experience that retains desktop use, not a separate native app.

## Brand Commitments

The existing product name is LeadFlow. The user-provided mobile UI reference is a binding layout and content direction for this work.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working web application, API integrations, role and permission controls, and a product logo at `client/public/logo.png`. The supplied reference image shows the intended mobile screen set and visual hierarchy. No external customer, performance, or conversion claims were provided.

## Product Principles

- Keep lead follow-up and today's work easy to reach.
- Preserve server-authoritative lead, dashboard, and activity data.
- Respect role-based visibility and permissions on every screen.
- Keep the existing desktop workflows available in the responsive web app.
